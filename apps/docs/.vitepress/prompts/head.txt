Rules:
- Portable head controller: createHeadController, set/remove patches, get snapshot, subscribe
- Browser: applyHead(document, snapshot); SSR: serializeHead(snapshot)
- React: HeadProvider / Head / useHead from @sometic/react/head
- Vue: provideHead / useHead from @sometic/vue/head
- Nestable patches by id; not a React-only Helmet fork; same engine across stacks
- Do not hardcode framework-only head APIs if the app may grow Vanilla/CE surfaces
